## 👨‍🔬 Research Focus

I am an AI researcher specializing in **neuromorphic computing** and **energy-efficient deep learning**. My research bridges biological neural principles with modern deep learning architectures to build efficient, brain-inspired AI systems. 

My current research focuses on **Spiking Vision Transformers**—developing lightweight architectures that combine the temporal dynamics of spiking neural networks with the representational power of attention mechanisms for event-based visual recognition.  This work aims to enable efficient deployment on neuromorphic hardware while maintaining competitive accuracy.

Previously, my M.Sc. thesis at **Shahid Beheshti University** (GPA: 3.7/4.0) established rigorous convergence guarantees for **spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP)** in recurrent spiking neural networks through Lyapunov stability analysis. This work demonstrated that STDP-based learning converges under specific conditions in recurrent architectures, validated experimentally on time-series classification benchmarks from the UCR archive.

My research combines **dynamical systems theory**, **neuromorphic computing**, and **efficient AI** to understand not merely what works empirically, but the fundamental principles governing adaptation in biologically-inspired neural systems.

> **Goal:** I seek a Ph.D. position to advance research on **spiking transformers**, **neuromorphic vision systems**, and **energy-efficient deep learning**. My aim is to develop theoretically-grounded, practically-deployable neuromorphic AI systems through open-source implementations and reproducible experiments.

## 🔬 Current Research

### SpikeLite: Lightweight Spiking Vision Transformer
*Ongoing Master's Thesis Research*

Developing an energy-efficient spiking transformer architecture for event-based visual recognition:
- **Lightweight Spiking Self-Attention (LSSA):** Novel attention mechanism with reduced computational complexity
- **Spike-Based Temporal Encoding:** Efficient representation of event camera data
- **Neuromorphic Hardware Compatibility:** Designed for deployment on Intel Loihi and similar platforms
- **Benchmarks:** DVS128 Gesture, N-Caltech101, CIFAR10-DVS
