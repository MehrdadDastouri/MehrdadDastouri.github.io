Research & Working Paper

Working Paper · SSRN 7755523

Distributionally Robust Reinforcement Learning for Dynamic Fee Mechanisms under Non-Stationary Order Flow in Automated Market Makers

Mehrdad Dastouri (Solo Author) · 2026 · Preprint & Extended Technical Monograph

Models dynamic swap-fee control for Uniswap v4 beforeSwap hooks as an oracle-free Robust MDP nested inside a sequential Stackelberg game with closed-form KKT CEX-DEX arbitrage clearing and empirically calibrated fee-elastic retail demand. Anchors fees on an uncapped validation-calibrated volatility floor (cbase = 20.0) with a bounded residual (±25 bps) trained via Joint 1-Wasserstein Residual DR-PPO, then distills the converged actor via L2-regularized ridge regression into a deterministic 15-coefficient O(1) polynomial. Calibrated to 15.8M 1-second Binance ETH/USDC observations and 31,252 Ethereum mainnet Uniswap v3 swaps (April–September 2024), ranking first under continuous adversarial state spoofing and transferring zero-shot to Layer-2 (2s and 1s) block cadences.

−57.5% Aug 2024 crash Net LVR vs. 5 bps static
−59.4% Maximum Drawdown reduction
94.1% Full-info oracle PnL (stationary, oracle-free)
<2,000 EVM gas via 15-coeff O(1) distillation
